a manifesto in eight parts
A chair announces what it is for before anyone sits in it.
02 Ornament is a debt. Someone always pays it later, usually the person cleaning it.
03 A material should look like what it is. Wood grain is not a mistake.
04 A design that needs a caption has failed at its first job.
05 Standardized parts are how a good idea gets to more people.
06 A workshop is not a classroom. Mistakes there cost material.
07 Color is a working part of a design, never a finishing touch.
08 The page, the chair, the building answer to the same questions.
Adopted by the Working Materials collective, spring session.
Eleven signatories. Copies posted at the workshop door.

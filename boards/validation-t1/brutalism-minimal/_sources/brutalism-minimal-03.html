Biography

I joined the Department of Statistics at Whitfield University in 2003. My work is in survival analysis and its applications to public health data, with a secondary interest in the history of statistical computing. Before Whitfield I was a postdoc at the Reyner Institute. I completed my PhD at Denholm University in 1999.

Research Interests

  • Survival analysis and censored data models
  • Semiparametric regression
  • Applications to epidemiology and public health
  • History and practice of statistical computing

The toner streak has been running down the left margin of every issue since volume one, and nobody on staff will fix it. The copier that makes it is older than half the contributors. Replacing the drum would remove the mark that half the readers now associate with the publication itself, so the copier stays.

OFFCUT does not proof digitally before print. Layout happens on a light table with actual scissors, actual tape, and an actual ruler that nobody trusts past the twelve inch mark. The web edition you are reading is a direct scan of those boards, reproduced at whatever size the boards happened to be that month, not a redesign for the browser.

Subscriptions do not exist. If you want the next issue, you go to the distro table and buy the next issue. The mailing list announces the table's location and nothing else, twelve words a month, no images attached. Contributors are paid in copies and in beer, in that order, and every masthead role rotates whenever somebody gets tired of it.

The type in this issue was set in whatever the copier could hold onto. Where a character dropped out, it stays dropped. Page numbers were rubber-stamped by hand, which is why issue three has two page nines and no page ten, and why nobody has ever asked us to fix it.

Riverline trains will run Dockside to Mill Street and Fenwick Square to Union Approach on their normal schedule. Between Mill Street and Fenwick Square, a replacement shuttle bus will run every twelve minutes, stopping at street level outside each station.

Crosstown service is not affected but should expect crowding at Fenwick Square during the shuttle transfer.

Lower Gallery, East Wing

the unremarked city

Sept 3 – Nov 16 Open Wed–Sun, 10 to 5
Gallery talk Oct 4, 6pm
Forty gelatin silver prints made across a decade of walking the same six blocks, returning to loading docks, transformer yards, and the backs of buildings that most residents never turn to look at directly.

## The thesis

### Values
- Flat grounds. Each screen has one solid field (white, off-white, black, or red) with no texture.
- Few colors. Most channels allow one accent or two colors at most per screen.
- Type does the structural work. Grotesk display sets hierarchy, and mono carries metadata.
- Low density. Low-density is the top tag (7), so screens hold little content.
- Rules over boxes. Thin gray hairlines divide content in the grid and case study channels.
- Muted chroma is the base. Hard color appears as a single mark, not as a wash.

### Visual laws (do)
- Give each screen one flat ground: #ffffff, #000000, #ffffcc, or #ff3300.

- Divide the grid with thin gray hairline rules (#cccccc), not borders or cards.
- Set display type in a grotesk at large size. Set labels, dates, and indexes in mono.

- Keep few items per screen and leave wide empty space around them.
- Use an index grid for lists of work, with numbered or labeled rows.
- On warm paper screens, pair #ffffcc with a faint #ffcccc or #cccccc secondary.
- On dark screens, use near-black (#000000 to #333333) and off-white text.
